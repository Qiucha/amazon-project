1 · What to focus on

Keep these quantities distinct. Regime and stability are labels on an equilibrium, not outcome metrics. Intensity of the rat race is the tutoring threshold Sd (or participation F(Sd)), summarized across groups by population tutoring mass.

Q

Quota — fraction of the applicant population the university can admit.

Model-controlled. Larger Q does not automatically mean less competition.

w

Diversity weight in university value v = (1−w)a + w d.

How much the university prefers the non-manipulable dimension d.

Sd · F(Sd)

Tutoring threshold and within-group participation for diversity group d.

Students with cost c ≤ Sd tutor. Physical “how fierce is gaming here.”

mass

Population tutoring mass — share of all applicants who tutor.

Primary intensity summary across both diversity groups.

Cd

Credibility — share of the observed high-academic pool with diversity d whose true a is 1.

Tutoring dilutes Cd: the high-score signal becomes less trustworthy.

μad · xad

Posterior evaluation (how the university ranks observed type ad) and admission probability.

μ drives ranking; x is who actually gets seats after filling Q.

adm a=1 · adm d=1

Shares of underlying a=1 and d=1 inside the admitted class.

Class composition — not the same as applicant stratum mass D₁.

(a, d)

Underlying type in {00, 01, 10, 11}. Tutoring flips observed a only; d cannot be faked.

University sees observed type after gaming, then ranks by μ.

At w = 0 the two-criterion engine nests the one-stage lab. Toggle quota: in the selected tight equilibrium, a larger class can raise tutoring intensity.

3 · Stratified rat race (w = 3/4)

Strong diversity preference makes ranking μ₁₁ > μ₀₁ > μ₁₀ > μ₀₀ tutoring-independent. Diversity chooses who races; competition in the marginal group remains. Quotas sit on opposite sides of D₁ = 0.36.

4 · Mixed tight-quota (w = 1/4)

Mild diversity weight: before tutoring, ranking is μ₁₁ > μ₁₀ > μ₀₁ > μ₀₀. At the selected mixed equilibrium the diversity premium is neutralized at the margin by gaming-induced credibility differences.

5 · Main takeaway

Across all six notebook (w, Q) points, valuing diversity in the objective alone does not eliminate the rat race. The mechanism changes by regime; intensity (mass / thresholds) does not vanish.

Writeup §5 Moving from a single criterion to a multi-criteria admission objective does not by itself eliminate the student rat race. Strategic responses reshape the informational content of the criteria and can partially neutralize the university’s intended diversity preference.
